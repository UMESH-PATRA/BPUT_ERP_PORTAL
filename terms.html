Terms of Service & Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Governing Institution: Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Odisha

1. Acceptance of Terms

By logging into, browsing, or utilizing the BPUT Digital Campus platform, enrolled students, faculty members, proctors, and authorized campus personnel agree to abide by these Terms of Service and applicable university statutes. If you do not agree to these terms, you must not access this system.

2. Credential Security & Prohibited Sharing

Your Student ID, Administrator ID, and corresponding cryptographic passwords represent official academic credentials. Sharing credentials with third parties or generating fraudulent gate pass requests constitutes an academic offense under the University Disciplinary Code.

  • Users must immediately report compromised credentials to helpdesk@bput.ac.in.
  • System access logs, including session times and IP ranges, are audited to prevent account hijacking.

3. Acceptable Campus Use & Disciplinary Policies

The portal serves legitimate academic and residential administration only. Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Submitting fabricated hostel maintenance complaints or false gate departure logs.
  • Attempting denial-of-service, automated scraping, SQL/XSS payload injection, or unauthorized administrative privilege escalation.
  • Any harassment, cyberbullying, or ragging-related intimidation, which is subject to immediate expulsion and criminal prosecution under Supreme Court directives.

4. Age Eligibility & Verification

Users must be at least 18 years of age or enrolled university scholars who have gained formal statutory admission to university programs. Minor users must have consent submitted by parents or legal guardians via official physical university admission documentation.

These Terms of Service are governed by the laws of India and the state statutes of Odisha (BPUT Act, 2002). Any disputes arising under these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Rourkela and the High Court of Orissa, Cuttack.
