Institutional Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Governing Body: Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Odisha

1. Principle of Data Minimization

The BPUT Digital Campus operates under strict data minimization standards. We collect only the information strictly necessary to authenticate academic identity, authorize digital gate passes, maintain hostel welfare, and record examination schedules.

  • Academic Identifiers: University Registration Number, Student ID, Branch, and Current Semester.
  • Residential Records: Assigned Hostel Hall and Room Number.
  • Operational Records: Exit time, destination, and return timestamps for campus gate passes.
  • Zero Unnecessary Data: We do NOT collect GPS location tracking, biometric data, social media profiles, or financial payment details on this platform.

2. Age Eligibility & Consent

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), services for adult learners require verifiable identity. This portal is intended for enrolled university scholars who are at least 18 years of age or enrolled students admitted under statutory university admission criteria. By creating an account or logging in, users affirm their compliance with university age standards.

3. Third-Party SDK Audit & Zero-Ad Guarantee

We conduct continuous technical audits of all client-side scripts. Our portal contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero commercial tracking beacons, and zero data brokers.

5. Right to Erasure & Data Deletion Request

Under Section 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was processed.

Important Statutory Exception: Official degree awards, enrolled registration indexes, and examination grades are statutory permanent academic records mandated by UGC and AICTE regulations, and cannot be expunged.
