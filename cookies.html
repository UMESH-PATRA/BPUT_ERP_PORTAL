Cookie Policy & Storage Transparency

Published: November 20, 2026 • Governing Body: Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), Odisha

1. What are Cookies and Storage Tokens?

Cookies and local storage tokens are small text records stored on your device when you browse web services. The BPUT Digital Campus platform uses local storage keys strictly to maintain secure student sessions, preserve UI theme choices, and verify proctorial authorizations.

2. Storage Categories on Our Platform

Strictly Necessary (Essential)

Required for the website to function. Includes session authentication tokens and route protection guards. Cannot be disabled without breaking portal login.

Preferences & Accessibility

Preserves your preferred visual display mode (Dark/Light mode) and language setting between visits.

3. Complete Storage Inventory Table

Token Key Category Purpose Duration
BPUT_AUTH_SESSION_V1 Essential Encrypted student/admin login state Session / Logout
campus_theme Preference Remembers Dark Mode or Light Mode 1 Year
BPUT_COOKIE_CONSENT_V1 Essential Records user cookie banner choice 1 Year
BPUT_UTM_DATA Attribution Anonymous portal entry channel Session Only
